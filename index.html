Hover the screen to read the intensity at a point. Relative intensity, 1 = one beam at its peak

Beams with exactly the same wavelength add as waves, so a 180° beam subtracts from them. Beams of different wavelengths never cancel; they add as light, even when they differ by 1 nm. Unequal intensities at 180° leave a residual.
